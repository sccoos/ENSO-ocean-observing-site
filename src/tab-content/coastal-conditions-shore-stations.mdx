<p class="text-muted-foreground"><strong>About the data:</strong> Automated shore stations on piers, wharves and other coastal sites (such as Humboldt, Santa Cruz Wharf and Scripps Pier) measure nearshore seawater temperature every few minutes, as part of the CeNCOOS and SCCOOS networks. Each plot compares recent readings with the station's long-term record: the average, the minimum and maximum, and the 90th percentile, which is the usual threshold for a marine heatwave. Data are quality-controlled following national (QARTOD) standards, and full records are available on the <a href="https://data.caloos.org">CalOOS Data Portal</a>.</p>
