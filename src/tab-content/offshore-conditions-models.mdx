  <p class="data-portal-embed__description">The West Coast Operational Forecasting System (<a class="underline underline-offset-2 hover:opacity-80" href="https://data.caloos.org/?ls=2i3VT67u#metadata/bd85b0d7-be99-45d3-9d1e-c14110170f16/44e71385-64de-44ea-a577-a8198252605d">WCOFS</a>) is an ocean forecasting model for the U.S. West Coast based on open-sourced Regional Ocean Monitoring System (ROMS). It brings together satellite observations, coastal radar, river data, weather forecasts, and ocean physics to estimate present-day conditions and forecast up to three days. The model's variables include temperature, salinity, currents, and sea level; it is currently developmental and intended for research and evaluation.</p>
