    <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-foreground">Coastal Upwelling Transport Index (CUTI)</h3>
    <p class="mt-3 text-muted-foreground">CUTI provides estimates of vertical transport near the coast (i.e., upwelling/downwelling). It was developed as a more accurate alternative to the previously available ‘Bakun Index’.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="rounded-xl border border-layer-line bg-layer p-5">
    <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-foreground">Biologically Effective Upwelling Transport Index (BEUTI)</h3>
    <p class="mt-3 text-muted-foreground">BEUTI provides estimates of vertical nitrate flux near the coast (i.e., the amount of nitrate upwelled/downwelled), which may be more relevant than upwelling strength when considering some biological responses.</p>
  </article>
